Cloudflare Turnstile setup for PO-Chem WebApp

1. In Cloudflare dashboard, open Turnstile and create a widget.
2. Widget name: PO-Chem WebApp. Widget type: Managed (recommended).
3. Add hostname: po-chem-webapp-request-ijpb.streamlit.app
4. Create the widget and copy its Sitekey and Secret key.
5. In Streamlit Cloud > app > Settings > Secrets, add:

TURNSTILE_SITEKEY = "your_public_sitekey"
TURNSTILE_SECRET = "your_private_secret"

Do not share or commit the secret key. Remove the old HCAPTCHA_SITEKEY and HCAPTCHA_SECRET entries once Turnstile is working.
6. Save Secrets and allow Streamlit to restart. Then reload the app with Ctrl+Shift+R.

Turnstile tokens are validated server-side through Cloudflare Siteverify.
